[PART 6 — MORE DETECTION]
Four more modules handle input you feed in. The log analyzer reads web and auth logs. It catches credential stuffing, recon sweeps, and injection payloads. Reverse shells, encoded commands, and ransomware-rate file activity. And the L-L-M defense module protects your own AI applications. It detects prompt injection, jailbreaks, and secrets leaking in model output. As AI gets woven into more apps, that attack surface only grows. Seven modules, one unified view of your risk.
